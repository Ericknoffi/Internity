How to spot a fake job posting
💰
Unrealistic salary
Salaries far above market rate for the role or experience level are a major red flag.
📱
Personal contact info
Legitimate companies use official email domains, not Gmail or WhatsApp for hiring.
⚡
Urgency language
"Apply now", "limited seats", "urgent hiring" — pressure tactics are common in scams.
📋
Vague requirements
"No experience needed", "anyone can apply" — real jobs have specific skill requirements.
🏠
Work from home bait
Scam postings heavily promote easy remote work with guaranteed high income.
🔍
No company info
Real companies include their name, website, and office location in job postings.
